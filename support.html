AntStreams Support

AntStreams is a media player for content supplied by your own authorized provider. The app does not provide a TV subscription.

For app support or privacy questions, email anthonyaldom1@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords or provider credentials.

Provider catalogs, stream availability, and playback rights are managed by your provider. For problems with your subscription or a particular stream, contact your provider.
